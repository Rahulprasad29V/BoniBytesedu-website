Top 10 AI Tools Every Student Should Use in 2026

Artificial Intelligence is changing the way students learn, write assignments, code and prepare presentations. Here are 10 AI tools every student should know.

  1. ChatGPT – Learn concepts, coding, writing and brainstorming.
  2. Google Gemini – Research and Google Workspace assistance.
  3. Microsoft Copilot – Word, Excel and PowerPoint AI.
  4. Canva AI – Create presentations and posters quickly.
  5. Grammarly – Improve grammar and writing.
  6. Notion AI – Smart notes and productivity.
  7. Perplexity AI – AI search with sources.
  8. Gamma AI – Generate presentations instantly.
  9. GitHub Copilot – AI coding assistant.
  10. Claude – Long-form writing and summaries.
